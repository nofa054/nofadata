## Daftar Proyek Portofolio

### 1. [Brazilian E-Commerce Sales & Logistics Analysis (Olist 100k Orders)](./projects/01-brazilian-ecommerce-sales-logistics)
- **Tools:** SQL, Python (`Pandas`), Interactive Dashboard
- **Ringkasan:** Menganalisis **98.666 pesanan selesai** senilai **R$ 15.84M** dari **95.420 pelanggan aktif** untuk memetakan tren penjualan bulanan (2017–2018), kategori produk & state terbesar, serta dampak keterlambatan pengiriman terhadap *Review Score* pelanggan (`4.21` On Time vs `2.54` Late Delivery).

### 2. [Marketing Funnel & Channel Performance Analysis (8.000 MQL Leads)](./projects/02-olist-marketing-funnel-analysis)
- **Tools:** SQL, Python, Funnel Analytics, CSV ETL
- **Ringkasan:** Evaluasi konversi **8.000 Marketing Qualified Leads (MQL)** ke **842 Closed Deals (10,53% Conversion Rate)** dan analisis kecepatan *Sales Cycle* (rata-rata 48,4 hari; median 14 hari) di 10 channel pemasaran.

### 3. [Customer Segmentation (RFM) & Retention Dashboard (Online Retail II UCI)](./projects/03-customer-segmentation-rfm-kmeans)
- **Tools:** SQLite, Python (`Scikit-Learn K-Means K=4`), Microsoft Excel, Pareto 80/20
- **Ringkasan:** Mengelompokkan **5.878 pelanggan unik** dengan total transaksi **£17.37M** ke dalam 4 segmen (*Champions*, *At Risk*, *Potential Loyalists*, *Hibernating*) serta membuktikan konsentrasi Pareto di mana **23,04% pelanggan teratas menyumbang 80% total revenue**.
